HOW IT WORKS

From Detection
to Response.

A connected emergency workflow designed to reduce the time between accident detection and emergency response.

01

Detect

Sensors continuously monitor the vehicle and identify abnormal impact conditions.

02

Analyze

The controller evaluates sensor data to determine whether an accident has occurred.

03

Alert

Accident information and GPS location are sent to the emergency response network.

04

Respond

Emergency departments receive the alert and coordinate the appropriate response.
